name: Chicken thighs with broccoli in the drippings and leftover rice
description: "One serving of skillet chicken thighs with broccoli sauteed in the drippings over leftover Instant Pot rice, with salsa. Butter only, never seed oils."
date: 2026-11-18
today: true
yield: "1 serving"
prep_time: PT5M
cook_time: PT25M
total_time: PT30M
category: Dinner
sitemap: false
robots: noindex, nofollow
tools:
  - Skillet
  - Instant-read thermometer
ingredients:
  - 10 oz boneless chicken thighs, thawed
  - Salt
  - Pepper
  - Garlic powder
  - Crushed red pepper
  - 1 teaspoon salted butter
  - 2 cups frozen broccoli florets
  - 1 to 2 tablespoons water
  - About 1/2 cup cooked Instant Pot rice from Tuesday
  - Salsa
steps:
  - name: Season the chicken
    text: >-
      Pat 10 oz thawed chicken thighs dry. Season them with salt, pepper,
      garlic powder, and crushed red pepper.
  - name: Melt the butter
    text: >-
      Heat a skillet over medium-high heat and melt 1 teaspoon salted butter.
      Use butter only, never seed oils.
  - name: Sear the first side
    text: >-
      Lay the thighs in the skillet and sear the first side for 5 to 6
      minutes without moving them. Start a 6-minute First side timer.
  - name: Sear the second side
    text: >-
      Flip the thighs and sear the second side for 5 to 6 minutes, until the
      thickest part reads 165 F. Start a 6-minute Second side timer.
  - name: Rest the chicken
    text: >-
      Move the chicken to a plate and let it rest for 5 minutes. Start a
      5-minute Rest timer.
  - name: Add the broccoli
    text: >-
      Add 2 cups frozen broccoli florets and 1 to 2 tablespoons water to the
      same skillet. Scrape up the browned bits. Never microwave the broccoli.
  - name: Saute the broccoli
    text: >-
      Stir the broccoli for 4 to 6 minutes, until it is tender with some
      browned edges. Start a 5-minute Broccoli timer.
  - name: Plate and serve
    text: >-
      Warm about 1/2 cup cooked rice for about 1 minute (the microwave is
      fine for the rice only) and put it in a bowl. Top with the chicken and
      broccoli, then add salsa.
---
