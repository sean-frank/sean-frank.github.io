  - Skillet
  - Toaster oven
  - Instant-read thermometer
ingredients:
  - 10 oz boneless chicken thighs
  - Garlic powder
  - Salt
  - Pepper
  - Crushed red pepper
  - 1 teaspoon salted butter
  - 2 cups frozen broccoli florets
  - About 1/2 cup leftover Instant Pot rice
steps:
  - name: Season the chicken
    text: >-
      Season 10 oz boneless chicken thighs with garlic powder, salt,
      pepper, and crushed red pepper.
  - name: Melt the butter
    text: >-
      Heat a skillet over medium-high heat and add 1 teaspoon salted
      butter. Use butter only, never seed oils.
  - name: Sear the first side
    text: >-
      Lay the thighs in the skillet and sear the first side for 6 to 7
      minutes. Start a 7-minute First side timer.
  - name: Sear the second side
    text: >-
      Flip the thighs and sear the second side for 6 to 7 minutes, until
      the chicken reaches 165°F. Start a 7-minute Second side timer.
  - name: Rest the chicken
    text: Take the chicken out of the skillet and let it rest.
  - name: Cook the broccoli
    text: >-
      Add 2 cups frozen broccoli florets to the drippings in the skillet
      and sauté for 6 to 8 minutes. Start an 8-minute Broccoli timer.
  - name: Reheat the rice
    text: >-
      Reheat about 1/2 cup leftover Instant Pot rice from Monday, covered,
      in the toaster oven or in a skillet with a splash of water, until the
      rice is hot. Skip the microwave if you can. The microwave is fine for
      the rice only.
  - name: Serve
    text: Serve the chicken with the broccoli and rice.
