name: Taco rice bowl
description: "One serving of taco-seasoned ground beef with onion and yellow bell pepper over Instant Pot rice, with roasted broccoli and salsa. The rice batch is larger; save the extra for Tuesday. Butter only, never seed oils."
date: 2026-11-16
today: true
yield: "1 serving"
prep_time: PT10M
cook_time: PT30M
total_time: PT40M
category: Dinner
sitemap: false
robots: noindex, nofollow
tools:
  - Instant Pot
  - Skillet
  - Toaster oven
ingredients:
  - 1 cup rice
  - 1 cup water
  - 6 oz 93/7 ground beef
  - 1/2 onion, diced
  - 1/2 yellow bell pepper, diced
  - 1 teaspoon salted butter
  - Taco seasoning
  - A splash of water
  - 2 cups frozen broccoli florets
  - Salsa
steps:
  - name: Dice the vegetables
    text: Dice 1/2 onion and 1/2 yellow bell pepper.
  - name: Load the rice
    text: >-
      Rinse 1 cup rice and put it in the Instant Pot with 1 cup water. This
      batch makes about 3 cups cooked rice. Use about 1/2 cup tonight and
      save the rest for Tuesday.
  - name: Cook the rice
    text: >-
      Seal the Instant Pot and cook on High pressure for 4 minutes. Start a
      4-minute Rice timer. When it beeps, leave the pot sealed for a
      10-minute natural release. Start a 10-minute Natural release timer.
      Do not quick-release.
  - name: Melt the butter
    text: >-
      While the rice cooks, heat a skillet and add 1 teaspoon salted
      butter. Use butter only, never seed oils.
  - name: Brown the beef
    text: >-
      Brown 6 oz 93/7 ground beef with the diced onion and yellow bell
      pepper for 6 to 8 minutes, until the beef reaches 160°F. Start an
      8-minute Beef timer.
  - name: Season the beef
    text: >-
      Add taco seasoning and a splash of water. Cook for 2 minutes. Start a
      2-minute Seasoning timer.
  - name: Roast the broccoli
    text: >-
      Heat the toaster oven to 425°F. Spread 2 cups frozen broccoli florets
      on a tray and roast for 15 to 18 minutes, flipping halfway. Start an
      18-minute Broccoli timer.
  - name: Assemble the bowl
    text: >-
      Scoop about 1/2 cup cooked rice into a bowl. Add the beef, onion,
      pepper, and broccoli, then spoon salsa on top. Refrigerate the
      leftover rice for Tuesday.
---
