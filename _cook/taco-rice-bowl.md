---
layout: recipe
name: Taco rice bowl
description: "One serving of taco-seasoned ground beef with onion, bell pepper, and broccoli cooked together on the stove, over Instant Pot rice with salsa. The rice batch is larger; save the extra for Wednesday. Butter only, never seed oils."
date: 2026-11-17
today: true
yield: "1 serving"
prep_time: PT5M
cook_time: PT25M
total_time: PT30M
category: Dinner
sitemap: false
robots: noindex, nofollow
tools:
  - Instant Pot
  - Skillet
  - Instant-read thermometer
ingredients:
  - 1 cup rice
  - 1 cup water
  - 6 oz 93/7 ground beef
  - 1/2 onion, diced
  - 1/2 bell pepper, diced
  - 1 teaspoon salted butter
  - 2 cups frozen broccoli florets
  - Salt, pepper, and garlic powder
  - Taco seasoning
  - 2 to 3 tablespoons water
  - Pace salsa
steps:
  - name: Load the rice
    text: >-
      Rinse 1 cup rice and put it in the Instant Pot with 1 cup water. This
      batch makes about 3 cups cooked rice. Use about 1/2 cup tonight and
      save 1/2 cup for Wednesday.
  - name: Cook the rice
    text: >-
      Seal the Instant Pot and cook on High pressure for 4 minutes. Start a
      4-minute Rice timer. When it beeps, leave the pot sealed for a
      10-minute natural release. Start a 10-minute Natural release timer.
      Do not quick-release.
  - name: Brown the beef
    text: >-
      Heat a skillet over medium-high heat and melt 1 teaspoon salted
      butter. Brown 6 oz 93/7 ground beef for 4 to 5 minutes, breaking it
      up, until it reaches 160°F. Start a 5-minute Beef timer.
  - name: Add the onion and pepper
    text: >-
      Add the diced onion and bell pepper. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes, until
      softening. Start a 3-minute Vegetable timer.
  - name: Cook the broccoli
    text: >-
      Add 2 cups frozen broccoli with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
      Stir, cover, and cook for 4 to 5 minutes, until tender-crisp. Start a
      5-minute Broccoli timer. Never microwave the broccoli.
  - name: Season the skillet
    text: >-
      Stir in taco seasoning and 2 to 3 tablespoons water. Simmer for 2 to
      3 minutes, until saucy. Start a 3-minute Seasoning timer.
  - name: Portion the rice
    text: >-
      Release the pressure and fluff the rice. Scoop about 1/2 cup into a
      bowl and about 1/2 cup into a container for Wednesday. Freeze the
      rest in 1/2-cup portions.
  - name: Assemble the bowl
    text: Spoon the beef and broccoli over the rice and top with Pace salsa.
---
