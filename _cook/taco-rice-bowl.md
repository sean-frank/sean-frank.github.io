name: Taco rice bowl
description: "One serving of taco-seasoned ground beef with onion and yellow bell pepper over Instant Pot rice, with broccoli cooked right in the beef skillet so it soaks up the taco seasoning, and salsa on top. The rice batch is larger; freeze the extra in 1/2-cup portions. Butter only, never seed oils."
date: 2026-11-24
today: true
yield: "1 serving"
prep_time: PT10M
cook_time: PT25M
total_time: PT35M
category: Dinner
sitemap: false
robots: noindex, nofollow
tools:
  - Instant Pot
  - Skillet
ingredients:
  - 1 cup rice
  - 1 cup water
  - 6 oz 93/7 ground beef
  - 1/2 onion, diced
  - 1/2 yellow bell pepper, diced
  - 1 teaspoon salted butter
  - Taco seasoning
  - About 1/4 cup water, in splashes
  - 2 cups frozen broccoli florets
  - Salsa
steps:
  - name: Dice the vegetables
    text: Dice 1/2 onion and 1/2 yellow bell pepper.
  - name: Load the rice
    text: >-
      Rinse 1 cup rice and put it in the Instant Pot with 1 cup water. This
      batch makes about 3 cups cooked rice. Use about 1/2 cup tonight and
      freeze the rest in 1/2-cup portions.
  - name: Cook the rice
    text: >-
      Seal the Instant Pot and cook on High pressure for 4 minutes. Start a
      4-minute Rice timer. When it beeps, leave the pot sealed for a
      10-minute natural release. Start a 10-minute Natural release timer.
      Do not quick-release.
  - name: Brown the beef
    text: >-
      While the rice cooks, melt 1 teaspoon salted butter in a large skillet
      over medium-high heat. Brown 6 oz 93/7 ground beef with the diced onion
      and yellow bell pepper for 6 to 8 minutes, until the beef reaches 160 F.
      Start an 8-minute Beef timer.
  - name: Steam the broccoli in the skillet
    text: >-
      Push the beef to one side. Add 2 cups frozen broccoli florets to the
      open side with a splash of water (about 2 tablespoons), then cover the
      skillet for 4 minutes until the broccoli is bright green and thawed.
      Start a 4-minute Broccoli timer.
  - name: Season and sear together
    text: >-
      Uncover, stir the beef and broccoli together, and add taco seasoning
      with another splash of water. Cook uncovered for 3 minutes, stirring
      now and then, until the liquid cooks off and the broccoli picks up a
      little char and the seasoning. Start a 3-minute Seasoning timer.
  - name: Assemble the bowl
    text: >-
      Scoop about 1/2 cup cooked rice into a bowl. Top with the beef and
      broccoli, then spoon salsa on top. Freeze the leftover rice in 1/2-cup
      portions.
