---
layout: recipe
name: Beef and rice bowl
description: "One serving of taco-seasoned ground beef with onion and broccoli cooked in the same skillet, over reheated leftover rice, with salsa on top. No new rice batch. Butter only, never seed oils."
date: 2026-11-25
today: true
yield: "1 serving"
prep_time: PT3M
cook_time: PT17M
total_time: PT20M
category: Dinner
sitemap: false
robots: noindex, nofollow
tools:
  - Skillet
ingredients:
  - 6 oz 93/7 ground beef, thawed in the fridge
  - 1/2 onion, diced
  - 1 teaspoon salted butter
  - 2 cups frozen broccoli florets
  - About 2 tablespoons water, plus a splash more
  - Salt and pepper
  - Taco seasoning
  - 1 cup cooked rice (two frozen 1/2-cup portions)
  - 2 tablespoons salsa
steps:
  - name: Prep
    text: >-
      Dice whatever onion is left (about 1/2). Take the thawed beef out of the
      fridge and set out two frozen 1/2-cup rice portions.
  - name: Brown the beef
    text: >-
      Melt 1 teaspoon salted butter in a skillet over medium-high heat. Brown
      the beef with the onion for 5 to 7 minutes, breaking it up, until the
      beef reaches 160 F. Start a 6-minute Beef timer.
  - name: Add the broccoli
    text: >-
      Push the beef to one side. Add 2 cups frozen broccoli with about 2
      tablespoons water and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  - name: Steam the broccoli
    text: >-
      Cover the skillet and cook for 4 minutes until the broccoli is bright
      green and thawed. Never microwave the broccoli. Start a 4-minute
      Broccoli timer.
  - name: Season
    text: >-
      Uncover and stir the beef and broccoli together with taco seasoning and
      a splash of water. Cook uncovered for 3 minutes until saucy. Start a
      3-minute Seasoning timer.
  - name: Reheat the rice
    text: >-
      Add the 1 cup frozen rice with a splash of water, cover, and heat for 3
      to 4 minutes until hot, stirring once. Start a 3-minute Rice timer.
  - name: Assemble the bowl
    text: >-
      Turn the heat off. Bowl the beef, broccoli, and rice, and top with 2
      tablespoons salsa.
---
