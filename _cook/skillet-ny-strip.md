name: Skillet NY strip with toaster-oven potato and roasted broccoli
description: "One serving of skillet NY strip with a toaster-oven potato and roasted broccoli. Start the potato in the microwave, then crisp it in the dual-door toaster oven. Butter only, never seed oils."
date: 2026-11-15

tools:
  - Microwave
  - Dual-door toaster oven
  - Cast-iron skillet
  - Instant-read thermometer
ingredients:
  - About 10 oz NY strip steak
  - 1/2 tablespoon salted butter
  - Salt
  - Pepper
  - Garlic powder
  - 1 small russet potato (about 225 g)
  - 2 cups frozen broccoli florets
steps:
  - name: Heat the oven and prep the potato
    text: >-
      Heat the toaster oven to 425°F. Poke holes all over 1 small russet
      potato (about 225 g).
  - name: Microwave the potato
    text: >-
      Microwave the potato for 5 minutes. Start a 5-minute Potato timer.
      Flip the potato, then microwave for 3 to 5 minutes more, until it is
      fork-tender. Start a 5-minute Potato flip timer.
  - name: Crisp the potato and roast the broccoli
    text: >-
      Put the potato in one toaster-oven door and crisp it at 425°F for
      10 to 12 minutes. Start a 12-minute Potato crisp timer. Spread 2 cups
      frozen broccoli florets on a tray and roast them in the other door at
      425°F for about 15 minutes, flipping halfway. Start a 15-minute
      Broccoli timer. Flip the florets when about 7 minutes remain. Never
      microwave the broccoli.
  - name: Season the steak and melt the butter
    text: >-
      Season about 10 oz NY strip on both sides with salt, pepper, and
      garlic powder. Set a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat and add
      1/2 tablespoon salted butter. Use butter only, never seed oils.
  - name: Sear the steak
    text: >-
      Lay the steak in the skillet and sear the first side for 3 to 4
      minutes. Start a 4-minute First side timer. Flip the steak and sear
      the second side for 3 to 4 minutes, until the center reads 130 to
      135°F for medium-rare. Start a 4-minute Second side timer.
  - name: Rest and serve
    text: >-
      Move the steak off the heat and let it rest for 5 minutes. Start a
      5-minute Rest timer. Slice the steak against the grain and serve it
      with the potato and broccoli.
