name: Skillet NY strip with toaster-oven potato and roasted broccoli
description: "One serving of skillet NY strip with a toaster-oven potato and roasted broccoli. Start the potato first in the dual-door toaster oven. Butter only, never seed oils."
date: 2026-11-15
today: false
yield: "1 serving"
prep_time: PT10M
cook_time: PT50M
total_time: PT1H
category: Dinner
sitemap: false
robots: noindex, nofollow
tools:
  - Dual-door toaster oven
  - Cast-iron skillet
  - Instant-read thermometer
ingredients:
  - About 10 oz NY strip steak
  - 1/2 tablespoon salted butter
  - Salt
  - Pepper
  - Garlic powder
  - 1 small russet potato (about 225 g)
  - 2 cups frozen broccoli florets
steps:
  - name: Start the potato
    text: >-
      Heat the toaster oven to 425°F. Poke holes all over 1 small russet
      potato (about 225 g) and put it in to bake for 45 to 50 minutes.
      Start a 50-minute Potato timer.
  - name: Season the steak
    text: >-
      Season about 10 oz NY strip on both sides with salt, pepper, and
      garlic powder.
  - name: Roast the broccoli
    text: >-
      When about 20 minutes remain on the Potato timer, spread 2 cups
      frozen broccoli florets on a tray. Use the toaster oven's other door
      so the potato keeps baking. Roast at 425°F for 15 to 18 minutes.
      Start an 18-minute Broccoli timer. Flip the florets when 9 minutes
      remain.
  - name: Melt the butter
    text: >-
      Set a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat and add 1/2 tablespoon
      salted butter. Use butter only, never seed oils.
  - name: Sear the first side
    text: >-
      Lay the steak in the skillet and sear the first side for 4 to 5
      minutes. Start a 5-minute First side timer.
  - name: Sear the second side
    text: >-
      Flip the steak and sear the second side for 4 to 5 minutes, until
      the center reads 130 to 135°F for medium-rare. Start a 5-minute
      Second side timer.
  - name: Rest the steak
    text: >-
      Move the steak off the heat and let it rest for 5 minutes. Start a
      5-minute Rest timer.
  - name: Serve
    text: Slice the steak and serve it with the potato and broccoli.
---
